**15. HoD's Responsibility Regarding Lesson Plans, Course Work Schedules, and Publishing Student Information**

**Policy Statement**

- The HoD shall ensure that lesson plans and course work schedules are prepared for every course and uploaded to the University website for transparency.

- Student information such as attendance, internal results, and academic progress shall be updated in the automation database and reflected on the website as per institutional policy.

**Actions by HoD**

- Lesson Plan & Course Schedule Preparation

<!-- -->

- Faculty prepare lesson plans with CO–PO mapping.

- HoD reviews, approves, and ensures timely upload to the university portal.

<!-- -->

- Academic Calendar & Website Publishing

<!-- -->

- Ensures semester-wise course work schedules (theory/lab/tutorial) are uploaded before commencement.

- Ensures real-time updates are visible to students, faculty, and stakeholders.

<!-- -->

- Student Information Updates

<!-- -->

- Directs faculty to upload attendance, internal exam results, and assignment scores to the ERP/automation system.

- Ensures consolidated data is periodically updated on the student information system (SIS) and website.

<!-- -->

- Monitoring & Verification

<!-- -->

- Conducts periodic audits to ensure all lesson plans and attendance data are uploaded without gaps.

- Verifies accuracy and consistency between database records and website updates.

**Evidence/Documents**

- Lesson plans and course schedules (faculty-wise, semester-wise).

- Screenshots of lesson plans/course work published on the university website.

- Attendance registers uploaded to ERP/automation system.

- Internal exam results & assignment score uploads in the system.

- Web logs/system reports showing last updated timestamps.

- Action Taken Reports (if discrepancies found in data uploads).

**17. HoD's Responsibility on Student Counseling and Disciplinary Matters**

**Policy Statement**

- The HoD is responsible for counseling students on academic matters, addressing disciplinary issues at the departmental level, and referring serious cases to the Institution's Disciplinary Committee (DC).

**Actions by HoD**

- Academic Counseling

<!-- -->

- Identifies slow/advanced learners based on internal assessments & attendance.

- Organizes remedial sessions, mentoring, and guidance on study methods, projects, and careers.

- Conducts one-on-one counseling sessions for at-risk students.

<!-- -->

- Disciplinary Oversight

<!-- -->

- Maintains decorum in classrooms, labs, and examinations.

- Handles minor misconduct (lateness, absenteeism, code of conduct violations) through warnings or counseling.

- For repeated or severe cases, escalates matter to the Departmental Discipline Cell or Institute DC.

<!-- -->

- Documentation & Reporting

<!-- -->

- Maintains counseling records (student mentoring sheets, feedback logs).

- Issues written warnings for minor disciplinary violations.

- Prepares reports and forwards cases to the Disciplinary Committee where necessary.

**Evidence/Documents**

- Student counseling/mentoring records (signed by counselor & HoD).

- Remedial class schedules & attendance.

- Academic progress reports (before vs after counseling).

- List of disciplinary cases handled at department level.

- Warning letters issued to students (if any).

**How the Support-First, Punish-Later Philosophy is Enforced**

- Support-First (Preventive Stage)

<!-- -->

- Provide multiple chances for students to improve.

<!-- -->

- Punish-Later (Corrective Stage)

<!-- -->

- DC handles severe/repeated violations with structured actions (probation, suspension, community service).

- ATRs ensure corrective measures are tracked and closed.

- → Evidence: DC referral letters, DC meeting minutes, ATRs.

- Counseling/mentoring records (signed by HoD & counselor).

- Remedial class attendance & progress reports.

- Academic progress data (before vs after intervention).

**Structured Escalation Path**

- Stage 1 – Counseling (Support-First Approach)

<!-- -->

- One-to-one or group mentoring sessions.

- Academic guidance (remedials, study skills, attendance improvement).

- Behavioral guidance for minor misconduct.

- → Evidence: Mentoring sheets, counseling records, remedial attendance.

<!-- -->

- Stage 2 – Warning (Corrective Signal)

<!-- -->

- If no improvement after counseling → HoD issues formal verbal/written warning.

- Warning letters recorded in departmental files.

- → Evidence: Warning letters, signed acknowledgement.

<!-- -->

- Stage 3 – Escalation to Disciplinary Committee (DC)

<!-- -->

- Severe/repeated cases forwarded to Institute DC.

- HoD prepares case report with supporting evidence.

<!-- -->

- Ensures activities do not clash with internal exams or critical academic schedules.

<!-- -->
